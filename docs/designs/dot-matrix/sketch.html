Time, date and the app name in Doto, a free dot-matrix font. Small labels in a typewriter-style monospace font.

Black, white and one red. Red only marks what's selected: the dot in the bar, the marker by the app name and the pips under the tile.

Tiles sit in black and white, and the selected one switches to full colour. The glow, gradient and frosted band are gone, and a faint dot grid fills the background.
