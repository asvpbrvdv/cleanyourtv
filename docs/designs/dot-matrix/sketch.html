Home

The dotted app name over the dock, its place in the dock under it, and red pips under the selected tile. Tiles stay black and white until selected.

App grid

Press Down from the dock and the grid slides up. The selected app's name appears under its tile with a red dot instead of the pips.

Inputs

Home dims and a panel drops from the bar. Filled dots are devices that are on, hollow ones are empty ports. The selected row turns solid with a red icon.

Settings

Hold OK on Settings to open. Number choices as boxes with a red dot under the current one; switches as rows with a red dot when on.

Mode button

The fourth segment in the top bar shows the current mode. OK cycles Dark, Light and Color. Colour mode is black with NYC Subway line colours as identifiers: numbered bullets for the dock, lettered ones for the grid.

Screensaver

A big dotted clock that drifts slowly around the screen so nothing burns in, with the date and one red dot.
